1. Who we are

Najeon Radio (나전 라디오) is a face-less Korean lofi / jazz YouTube channel that publishes AI-generated audio and visuals. The OAuth application associated with Google Cloud project najeon-radio is used only to manage and upload content to the channel owned by 손영태 (Youngtae Son) / Najeon Radio.

This app is not a consumer product offered to the general public. It is an internal publishing tool for the channel operator.

2. What the app does

With the channel owner's Google account consent, the app uses the YouTube Data API via OAuth 2.0 to:

  • Upload videos to the Najeon Radio YouTube channel
  • Manage channel videos, metadata, thumbnails, and related YouTube resources needed for publishing
  • Read channel/account information required to perform those actions

OAuth scope used (as configured in the client):
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube
(YouTube account access sufficient to upload and manage the authorized channel.)

The app does not request unrelated Google scopes (for example Gmail, Drive, Contacts, or Calendar) unless that is later documented and disclosed here.

3. What data we access and use

Through YouTube OAuth, the app may access and process:

CategoryExamplesPurpose
YouTube channel identity Channel ID, channel title, handle Confirm the authorized channel and publish to it
Video & publishing data Uploaded media, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, privacy status, playlists as needed Upload and manage videos
OAuth credentials Access token, refresh token, expiry Keep the authorized session working for uploads/management

We do not collect end-user viewer personal data through this OAuth app. Viewers interacting with the public YouTube channel are subject to YouTube's / Google's own privacy policies, not this document.

4. How tokens and data are stored

  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the channel operator's assistant computer used for publishing (file path example used by the tooling: ~/.najeon_youtube_token.json, permissions restricted where supported).
  • Tokens are used only to call YouTube APIs for the Najeon Radio channel.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal or channel data to third parties.
  • We do not use OAuth-accessed YouTube data for advertising profiling or for unrelated products.

If token storage later moves to a different machine, vault, or cloud secret store, this section and the effective date will be updated.

5. Sharing with third parties

  • Google / YouTube: API requests and OAuth flows are processed by Google under Google's terms and privacy policy.
  • No sale of data: We do not sell user or channel data.
  • Service providers: None beyond Google/YouTube and the static host of this policy (GitHub Pages, when this site is published there).

6. AI-generated content (channel disclosure)

Najeon Radio publishes AI-generated music and related visuals at a channel level. This privacy policy covers the OAuth publishing app, not the creative process itself. Content labeling on YouTube (for example AI-disclosure fields or descriptions) is handled in video/channel settings as required by platform rules.

7. Data retention

  • OAuth tokens are kept only while the channel operator needs automated upload/management. Tokens can be revoked in the Google Account security settings and by deleting the local token file.
  • Uploaded videos and metadata remain on YouTube under the channel owner's Google account until the owner deletes or changes them.

8. Your choices (channel owner)

The Google account that authorizes the app can:

  • Revoke access at any time via Google Account → Security → Third-party access
  • Delete the local token file on the assistant computer
  • Request questions about this policy using the contact below

Because this app serves a single channel operator, it is not designed for multi-user signup or public account registration.

9. Children

This OAuth app is not directed at children. It is used only by the adult channel operator.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when scopes, storage, or contact details change. The "Last updated" date above will be revised. Material changes should be reflected before relying on the new behavior in production OAuth.

  • 나전 라디오 채널에 동영상 업로드

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube
(승인된 채널에 업로드·관리할 수 있는 YouTube 계정 접근)

토큰 저장 위치를 다른 기기·금고·클라우드 시크릿으로 옮기면 이 절과 시행일을 갱신합니다.
